Wind, pink noise and brown noise are generated locally with Web Audio in `study-space.js`. They are labelled as synthesized ambience in the UI. They are not recordings, brainwave therapy, or medical interventions. No remote sound service or YouTube download is used. The supplied café artwork is reused as provided, with video resized/encoded for delivery; no new AI imagery or audio was generated in this implementation. Rain on the café's left window area is a CSS effect; the video itself shows a sunlit interior. Jazz Lofi remains a user-selected YouTube video using the existing player.
