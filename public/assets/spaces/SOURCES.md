Wind, pink noise and brown noise are generated locally with Web Audio in `study-space.js`. They are labelled as synthesized ambience in the UI. They are not recordings, brainwave therapy, or medical interventions. No remote sound service, YouTube download, or AI-generated image/audio asset is used.
